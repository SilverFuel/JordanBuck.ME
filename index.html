ABOUT

What I do

I run Tier 3 for the Indiana Office of Technology — the mechanics keeping the place running. The tickets that beat everyone else end up with us, and so do the projects that quietly change how the whole enterprise manages and maintains its devices.

A lot of that right now is modernization: moving off legacy, serial-based device management to a user-based Intune and Autopilot model. It looks tidy on a slide. In practice it rewrites how thousands of devices get handled, and the edge cases are where the real work lives — like the time a modern app rollout quietly ate the network because Microsoft's Delivery Optimization policy is easier to sidestep than it should be. We tracked it down with the networking team, fixed it with a Microsoft engineer, and made it boring again. That's the job: push things forward, own it when the edge cases bite.

Known for Taking the messy, enterprise-wide problems nobody wants and turning them into something that actually ships — and runs quietly afterward.
Current focus Endpoint modernization — Intune, Autopilot, the shift to user-based management — and dragging AI and automation into the day-to-day so the team spends less time on the same problems twice.
