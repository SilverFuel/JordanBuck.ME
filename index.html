ABOUT

What I do

I run Tier 3 for the Indiana Office of Technology. We take the escalations other teams cannot close, figure out what actually broke, and keep the same issue from coming back.

A lot of the work right now is moving thousands of Windows and Mac devices from legacy, serial-based management to Intune and Autopilot. The slide deck version looks simple. The real work is in the edge cases.

We saw that firsthand when a modern app rollout started swallowing network capacity. My team worked with networking and a Microsoft engineer, fixed the Delivery Optimization issue, and got the rollout back under control.

Known for Sticking with the ugly problems until they are fixed, documented, and no longer waking somebody up.
Current focus Intune, Autopilot, better ServiceNow workflows, and practical AI that saves the team time.
