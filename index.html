What I do

I'm an enterprise IT leader in Indianapolis who runs Tier 3 operations for Indiana Office of Technology, serving 70+ government agencies and thousands of endpoints. I lead a 20-person technical team and own the outcomes of the hardest escalations — root-cause analysis, endpoint modernization, and the move from legacy management to Intune and Windows Autopilot. I work where people, process, and technology meet, and I bring practical AI and automation into that work to make it faster, clearer, and repeatable.

Known for Leading complex IT initiatives end to end, modernizing endpoint and escalation operations, and translating ambiguous technical problems into clear, accountable execution plans — increasingly with AI and automation handling the repetitive work.
